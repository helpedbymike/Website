04 — The craft

Made to be kept.

i

Gold thread, not gold ink

The left-chest crest is a raised satin-stitch embroidery in metallic gold — 9,400 stitches, edge-bound so it never frays out of shape.

ii

Back print that survives

A 12-inch water-based discharge print that sinks into the fibre instead of sitting on top of it. Softer on day one, still there on year five.

iii

Weight you can feel

240 GSM ringspun cotton on the tee. 450 GSM brushed fleece on the hoodie. A waxed shell with a quilted lining on the jacket.

iv

Garment dyed, pre-shrunk

Dyed after construction so the colour settles into the seams, then washed down so the fit you buy is the fit you keep.

The creed

Nobody hands you custody of your life. You take it. You take it on the mornings that argue with you, in the years nobody claps for, in the rooms where it would be easier to let go of the wheel.

Family. Discipline. Purpose. Faith. Freedom. Passion. Growth. Legacy. Eight ways of saying the same thing — this one is mine, and I am not putting it down.
